Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026-11-01 · BSB ACCOUNTING LTD, 7200 Razgrad, Bulgaria

Who is responsible

The controller of your data is: BSB ACCOUNTING LTD, 7200 Razgrad, Bulgaria.
Contact for all privacy questions: bsbconsult25@gmail.com.

What is stored on your device

Your progress, coins, items, settings, nickname, avatar and language are stored on your device only (local storage). They stay there unless you use the online features below. You can delete them at any time in Settings → Delete my data.

What is sent to our servers

When your device is online, the game keeps a private cloud save for you, so that your progress is not lost if you change phone: an anonymous account ID (created automatically, without a name, e-mail address or phone number), your nickname and avatar, your progress (stars, coins, items, lives), your settings and your clan membership. Only you can read your cloud save. Other players can see part of it only if you use clans or the rankings: if you join a clan, your nickname, avatar and frame, total stars, highest level, the stars you collect for the clan each week and, for card swaps, the cards you want and your spare cards; if you turn on the world ranking (off by default, and “Hide me” turns it off again), your nickname, avatar and frame, total stars, highest level and this week's stars. When a weekly clan season ends, its result is stored on our servers: the clan standings and, for the winning clan, the nicknames and weekly stars of its members. Please never use your real name as a nickname. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, advertising ID or device identifiers.

Reports and blocking

Nicknames and clan names pass through a filter, and every player can report or block another player. A report stores who reported whom, the reason and the date, so that we can review it; we use it only to keep the game safe. Your list of blocked players is part of your cloud save. Contact: bsbconsult25@gmail.com.

Purchases

Purchases of coins are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We receive a confirmation that a purchase happened, but never your card or payment details.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no profiling. We never sell your data and never share it for advertising.

Why we use data and on what basis

To run the game (saving progress, clans, purchases): performance of the contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. To keep the game secure and prevent abuse: legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Who receives data

Google (Firebase: anonymous sign-in and the database for the online features, stored in the European Union), and Apple and Google for purchases and app distribution. If data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. When the game runs in a web browser, fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address.

Children

Anyone can play this game. We do not ask for your age or date of birth, and we do not collect real names, e-mail addresses or phone numbers: online features use only the nickname and avatar you choose and your game progress. Please never use your real name as a nickname. There is no chat, no advertising and no tracking for anyone. If you are under the age of majority in your country, ask a parent or guardian before making purchases with real money (see the Terms). Parents can ask us to delete a child's data at bsbconsult25@gmail.com.

How long we keep data and how to delete it

Data on your device stays until you delete it. Cloud data stays while you use the game. To delete everything, open Settings → Delete my data: this removes your data from your device and your cloud save, ranking entry and clan membership from our servers. You can also ask for deletion at bsbconsult25@gmail.com. When you write to us, include the profile ID shown at the bottom of Settings: it is the only way for us to find your cloud data, because that data is stored without your name or e-mail address.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your data, object to processing and withdraw consent. You may complain to your national data protection authority. Write to bsbconsult25@gmail.com.

Security

We use encrypted connections, and access rules make your cloud save readable only by you. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not put personal details in nicknames or clan names.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the current version, and important changes will be announced in the game.
